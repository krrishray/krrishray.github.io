2026.07 What RAG actually does, in plain terms
Retrieval-Augmented Generation sounds more complicated than it is: instead of relying only on what a language model already "knows," you retrieve relevant chunks of your own data first, then hand those to the model alongside the question. The model's job shifts from recalling facts to reasoning over what's in front of it. Notes from working through this in a bootcamp module — including where vector search fits in and why grounding matters for accuracy.
